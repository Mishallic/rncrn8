# Plan: a paid weekly briefing for independent dental clinics

## What we will sell

A weekly email briefing for owners of independent dental clinics in the UK: one page on
regulation changes, supplier price moves and staffing, written by a former practice manager.
Price: £29 a month, or £290 a year.

## Who buys it

There are about 12,000 dental practices in the UK. We assume 5% of them subscribe in the first
year, which is 600 clinics. At £29 a month that is £208,800 a year in revenue.

## How we reach them

We will buy a list of clinic owners' email addresses from a data broker and send a free sample
issue to all of them in the first week. We expect 20% to open it and 10% of those to subscribe.

## Costs

The writer costs £2,500 a month. Email software costs £50 a month. Card fees are 3%. We break
even at 90 subscribers.

## Timeline

Week 1: hire the writer. Week 1: send the sample issue to the full list. Week 2: launch paid
subscriptions. Month 3: reach 600 subscribers.

## Risks

The main risk is that the writer leaves. We will keep two issues written in advance.
